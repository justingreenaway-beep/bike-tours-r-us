Ride Together

Help and support

Plan and ride group motorbike tours: the route, bakeries, beds, dinners and the crew in one place.

Need a hand? Email ridetogethermototours@gmail.com. Tell us your tour’s name and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Getting started

Signing in

Open Ride Together and tap Create account with the email your organiser has on the crew list. If you rode with us in 2026, use the same email and password.

Forgotten your password?

On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap Forgot password. Check your junk folder if the email doesn’t arrive.

Can’t see your tour?

Tours appear once an organiser adds your email to the crew list and publishes the tour. Check that the email matches exactly, then reopen the app.

Planning a tour

  • Choose + Plan a new tour from the tour menu at the top. You’ll be the tour’s owner.
  • Add your riders in Plan → Crew. Set someone as an Editor and they can help plan.
  • Editors need a verified email. If Plan asks you to check your email, tap Send the link, open the email, tap the link, then tap I’ve tapped the link.
  • Turn off features a tour doesn’t need, such as Koala Court or Nights, in Plan → Features.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a photo: open it on the photo wall and tap Report.
  • Report a Koala Court charge: tap Report next to the charge.
  • Reports go to the tour’s editors, who can remove the photo or charge. The person isn’t told who reported it.
  • Block someone: tap their name anywhere in the app, then Block. You won’t see their photos, bakery finds or charges. Unblock them in your Profile.
  • Something serious or urgent? Email us as well (above).

Your account and privacy

  • Change your details: tap your initial (top right) to open your Profile.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete my account. You’ll be asked for your password. This can’t be undone.
  • Read the privacy policy for what the app keeps and who can see it.

Something not working?

  • Check for an update: Profile → App version → Check.
  • Maps, weather and place searches need a mobile signal. The roadbook keeps working offline once it’s loaded.
  • Still stuck? Email us with a screenshot if you can.
