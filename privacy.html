Ride Together

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Ride Together is an app for planning and riding group motorbike tours. This policy explains what information the app holds about you, who can see it, and how to delete it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Whether your email has been verified (needed to edit tours).

Tours you plan or join

  • Your name and email on a tour’s crew list, added by the tour’s organisers, and your role (owner, editor or rider).
  • What organisers enter to plan a tour: routes, stops, bakeries, beds and room allocations, dinners, roles and the tour brief.

Your profile (all optional)

  • Nickname, photo, colour, bike details, mobile number (and whether to show it), room and dietary preferences, your bike’s fuel range and preferred navigation app.
  • Safety details: an emergency contact (name, relationship, phone) and medical notes. These are only stored if you enter them, and are shown to everyone on the tours you’re on, so they can help in an emergency.

What you do on a tour

  • Bakery ticks and ratings, trivia scores, best-leg votes, photo likes, your pre-ride checklist, and Koala Court charges you lay or rule on.
  • Photos you post to the photo wall, with your name, the stop and the time.
  • Bakeries you add on the road, and reports you send about photos or charges.
  • When someone taps “We’re here”, the app records which planned stop the group has reached. It does not track your phone’s location.

On your phone only

Settings such as light or dark mode, the last tab you used, cached weather and which updates you’ve already seen are kept on your phone and are not sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no selling or renting of your information, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or profiling tools.
  • No GPS location tracking.

Who can see your information

  • Your crew: people on the same tour see your name, your profile (including safety details if you add them), your photos, scores and charges.
  • The tour’s editors: also see the crew list with emails, booking notes and any reports sent to them.
  • The app’s administrators: can see every tour to look after the app and help with problems.
  • People you block: blocking hides their photos and charges from you. It doesn’t hide your information from them.

Services the app uses

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts and tour information. Its servers may be outside Australia. The app also loads Firebase’s sign-in code from Google’s website for email verification and account deletion.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s files.
  • When you use these features, the app sends the place or search details (not your name or email) to:
    • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: searching for towns, bakeries, places to stay and eat; map pictures.
    • Open-Meteo: town search and weather forecasts for the stops.
    • FOSSGIS routing (OpenStreetMap): working out distances and riding times between stops.
  • Tapping Navigate or a Google Maps link opens Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps, which have their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open and the tours you’re on exist. You can change your profile at any time in the app.

To delete your account: open the app, tap your initial (top right) to go to your Profile, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in,
  • your profile, including safety details,
  • your ticks, ratings, scores, votes, likes and the Koala Court charges you laid,
  • the photos and bakeries you added,
  • and, if you choose, the tours you own.

Some things can remain because they belong to other people’s records: your name and email on a tour organiser’s crew list (until they remove you), charges other riders laid against you, and reports you sent to editors.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules only let people on a tour see it, only its editors change it, and editors must have a verified email.

Children

Ride Together is for adults riding motorbikes and is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell riders in the app.

Contact

Questions, a request to see or correct your information, or a privacy complaint: email contact email to be added.

If you’re not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
